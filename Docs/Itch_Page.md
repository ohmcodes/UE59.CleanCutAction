### About the game

**Clean! Cut! Action!** is a cozy first-person tidying game set on a two-floor New York cafe movie set. You play the Rookie, a production assistant on the last day of the shoot. Every scattered prop has a glowing silhouette showing exactly where it belongs. Pick it up, line it up, and snap it back into place.

But be careful: drop a prop beside its mark and it slips and shatters. Throw it and it breaks for sure. Every broken prop costs you points.

### Features

- **Put the set back together.** Chairs, tables, cups, plates, pastries and more, each with its own spot on the set.
- **Glowing tape marks.** Blue shows a prop's spot, yellow means you're lined up, orange means something underneath has to go back first (the table before the cup).
- **Things break.** Careless drops and throws smash props into pieces.
- **Two ways to play.** Race the clock in **Time Trial** for bonus points, or **Just Clean** with no timer and take it easy.
- **Hints when you're lost.** Highlight the nearest stray prop and follow the arrow.
- **Online leaderboard.** Enter your name and see how your take ranks against other players.
- **Plays in your browser** on desktop and touch devices, with keyboard and mouse, gamepad, or on-screen controls.

### Scoring

- **+10** for each prop back on its mark
- **-15** for each prop you break
- **Time Trial:** +1 for every second left on the clock

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard / Mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D / Arrows | Left stick |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Pick up / Place / Drop | Left click / E | X |
| Throw (breaks it!) | Right click | Right trigger |
| Hint | F | Y |
| Pause | P / Esc | Start |

On touch devices, use the on-screen sticks to move and look, and tap the hand button to pick up and place.

### Tips

- Aim right at the silhouette before you place. The hand icon turns red if the drop will break the prop.
- Put furniture back first. Anything that sits on top of it has to wait.
- The web build needs a browser with **WebGPU** support (recent Chrome or Edge recommended). Give it a moment to load on the first run.
